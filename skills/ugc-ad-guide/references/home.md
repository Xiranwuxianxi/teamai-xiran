## Auto Egg Dispenser · Denial Hook · Before/After Comparison

**Product**: Auto Egg Dispenser | 24-egg capacity | Transparent design | Auto-rolling mechanism
**Routine**: Trash the old → satisfying new → fridge space comparison
**BGM**: Silent first 7s (ASMR rolling eggs), then light beat
**Character**: American male, 20-30s, casual
**Setting**: Kitchen, open fridge, bright lighting

```
Voiceover: "Stop wasting fridge space with those bulky cardboard cartons! This automatic dispenser is a total game changer — it's so satisfying to use and keeps your eggs organized and easy to reach. It holds twenty-four eggs and looks so much sleeker in your fridge. You need this! Link in my bio to grab yours!"

(0-3s) [HOOK — Trash the Old]
Standing in front of an open fridge. Holding an ugly, bulky, slightly torn cardboard egg carton. Disgusted frown. Aggressively toss it into a trash can or shove it off-screen.
SFX: Carton toss thud.

(3-7s) [ASMR DEMO — Rolling Mechanism]
Extreme close-up (ASMR): Place eggs one by one into the top tier of the transparent dispenser. Then remove one egg from the bottom — the eggs above smoothly roll down to fill the gap. Capture the gentle rolling and clicking sounds.
SFX: Eggs rolling + soft clicking (KEY ASMR moment — must be crisp).

(7-11s) [SPACE COMPARISON]
Smoothly slide the loaded dispenser into the fridge shelf. Deliberately place a large milk jug next to it — visual contrast showing how slim and space-efficient it is compared to the old carton.
SFX: Fridge shelf sliding.

(11-15s) [CTA]
Back to main shot. Casually pull one egg from the dispenser with one hand. Raise an eyebrow / smile at camera. Other hand points down to the link.
SFX: Egg rolling click.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: Denial/anger ("Stop wasting...") + physical toss action — leverages loss aversion
- Wow Moment: Eggs rolling down to fill the gap — the ASMR sound is deeply satisfying
- Sound Strategy: Pure ASMR for the egg-rolling mechanism. No music during the key demo
- Key Takeaway: The egg-rolling ASMR is the entire sell. The milk jug comparison is clever — shows space savings without needing split screen

## 150-Inch Projector Home Cinema · Build-Up Ritual Flow · ASMR-to-Explosion Arc

**Product**: 150-Inch Projector + Screen Setup | Home cinema experience
**Routine**: Silent ASMR build-up → explosive payoff at screen turn-on
**BGM**: ABSOLUTE SILENCE for first 11 seconds (only processed ASMR sounds). Epic cinematic music EXPLODES at 11s when screen lights up
**Character**: Two people for the build montage
**Setting**: Living room, evening/night. Main light source = the projector screen itself

```
Voiceover: (No traditional voiceover — actions and sounds tell the story. Optional text overlay at 15s: "Never Leaving Home.")

(0-3s) [THE BUILD — Giant Screen Arrival]
Two people carry the massive 150-inch screen frame into the room. Synchronized steps. Mount it on the wall — wide-angle low shot emphasizing the enormous size.
SFX: Heavy footsteps (Thud-Thud) + metal bracket click-clack (no music — pure physical weight).

(3-6s) [THE SETUP — Precision]
Close-up: the projector (black metal box) placed on the coffee table. Hands plug in power cable and HDMI.
Camera: Extreme close-up on the lens glass and metal housing.
SFX: Cable snap-in + fingers sliding over metal casing (ASMR precision feel).

(6-9s) [THE RITUAL — Snack Prep]
Pull open a drawer under the coffee table — neatly stocked with snacks and drinks. Pick up a can, finger hooks the pull tab.
Camera: Overhead shot of the stocked drawer.
SFX: Drawer slide + can crack open (Psshh-Crack! — most satisfying sound).

(9-11s) [THE TRIGGER — Power On]
Sink into an electric recliner sofa. Thumb presses the remote power button.
Camera: Side medium shot capturing the sofa wrapping around the body.
SFX: Leather compression + deep bass power-on thrum.

(11-15s) [VISUAL EXPLOSION]
150-inch screen BLAZES to life! Vibrant 4K game landscapes fill the entire wall. Room ambient lights change color in sync (Ambilight effect). Quick cuts synced to music.
SFX: BGM EXPLODES — epic cinematic or heavy bass trap. In-game combat/engine sounds layered in.

(15-18s) [IMMERSION]
Camera pulls back: silhouette in the glow-filled room, gaming with a controller, body swaying with the action. Text: "Never Leaving Home."
SFX: BGM continues at peak, then fades.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: The sheer scale — two people carrying a 150-inch screen is physically impressive
- Wow Moment: Screen blazing to life at 11s — the silence-to-explosion audio arc
- Sound Strategy: 11 seconds of DEAD SILENCE with only hyper-processed ASMR (footsteps, metal, bubble sounds). Then BGM detonation. This contrast is the entire emotional architecture
- Key Takeaway: No voiceover needed — the ritual build-up creates anticipation naturally. The snack drawer is a lifestyle touch that makes viewers want the ENTIRE setup, not just the projector
