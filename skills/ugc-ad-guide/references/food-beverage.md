## Top Ramen Upgrade Hack · Recipe/Life Hack Flow · Curiosity Hook

**Product**: Top Ramen | $0.50 | Instant noodles
**Routine**: Recipe hack — upgrade cheap food into gourmet
**BGM**: Lo-fi Hip Hop or warm piano

```
Voiceover: "Let's upgrade this fifty-cent ramen. The secret? Mayo, garlic, and an egg yolk. Trust me on this. Mix it up until it's creamy and golden. Best late-night snack ever. You have to try this."

(0-3s) [HOOK — ASMR Unpackaging]
Rip open the Top Ramen packet. Pull out the noodle brick. Close-up: crispy texture of the dry noodle block.
SFX: Packaging tear (Riiip) + noodle brick crunch.

(3-7s) [THE HACK — Secret Ingredients]
Quick cuts: 1. Cooked noodles lifted from pot (no broth). 2. Stir in seasoning packet, a spoonful of mayo, a spoonful of minced garlic. 3. Drop a raw egg yolk right in the center.
SFX: Mayo squeeze (Squish) + egg yolk sliding in (Plop).

(7-11s) [MIXING — Visual Payoff]
Chopsticks pierce the egg yolk. Stir vigorously — noodles transform into thick, golden, sauce-coated creamy goodness.
SFX: Wet stirring sounds (thick and satisfying).

(11-15s) [EATING — The Finale]
Lift a massive chopstick-load of noodles, steam rising. Close-up of mouth slurping. Satisfied expression (or thumbs up if no face).
SFX: Slurp + "Mmm~" (satisfied sigh).
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: Curiosity-based ("upgrade this $0.50 ramen") — low barrier, high intrigue
- Wow Moment: Egg yolk breaking + noodles turning creamy golden
- Sound Strategy: Full ASMR — ripping, squishing, stirring, slurping. BGM stays quiet, food sounds dominate
- Key Takeaway: Recipe hacks perform well — viewers feel they learned something. "Trust me on this" builds parasocial trust

## Monster Rehab Tea + Lemonade 15-Pack · Bulk Haul Flow · Excitement Hook

**Product**: Monster Rehab Tea + Lemonade | 15-Pack | Non-carbonated, electrolytes, 25 cal
**Routine**: Bulk haul discovery — found-the-deal energy
**BGM**: Upbeat energy, light background
**Character**: American male, 20-30s, casual
**Setting**: Car interior POV (classic American TikTok format)

```
Voiceover: "Okay, I finally found the fifteen-pack of the best Monster flavor. This is the Rehab Tea and Lemonade. It's non-carbonated, packed with electrolytes, and only twenty-five calories. Literal game changer. Stop overpaying at the gas station. Grab the case right here before it's gone!"

(0-2s) [HOOK — Heavy Drop]
Slam the 15-pack box onto table or car seat. Quick zoom into the "Rehab" branding.
SFX: Heavy box thud.

(2-5s) [INTRO — Can Reveal]
Yank a single can from the box. Spin to show the yellow "Tea + Lemonade" label. Crack it open.
SFX: Crisp can-opening sound (PSSHH-CRACK!).

(5-10s) [FEATURES — Pour & Texture]
Pour into an ice-filled glass — smooth tea-colored liquid, no fizz. Or shake the can to show it's non-carbonated.
SFX: Liquid pouring over ice + ice rattling.

(10-15s) [CTA — Box Shot]
Camera pulls back to the full 15-pack. Finger taps frantically at bottom of screen (shopping cart area). Or slap the box to show it's stocked.
SFX: Tapping + energetic music swell.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: "Finally found it" energy — scarcity + discovery = dopamine
- Wow Moment: Smooth pour into ice (visual contrast with typical fizzy energy drinks)
- Sound Strategy: Car environment is naturally intimate. Can-opening crack is the ASMR anchor
- Key Takeaway: Car POV is uniquely American TikTok — feels like spontaneous "just bought this" moment. "Gas station" anchors to real-life purchasing
