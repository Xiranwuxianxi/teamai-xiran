## Linen Blend Shirt · Try-On/Haul Flow · Excitement Hook

**Product**: Linen Blend Shirt | Breathable, textured fabric
**Routine**: Try-on haul — showing off a new purchase with infectious excitement
**BGM**: Very low background music. Keep human voice loud and clear. Preserve fabric sounds (slapping, zipping) for authenticity
**Character**: Female blogger, energetic and expressive. Vacation vibe — the shirt looks expensive but feels comfortable
**Setting**: Living room with natural window light. Authentic home environment

```
Voiceover: "Oh my gosh, look at this texture! It's a linen blend but it is NOT scratchy at all! It's so breezy and cooling. I feel like I'm on vacation in Italy wearing this. The color is amazing. You need this for summer. Seriously, click the link!"

(0-4s) [HOOK — Fabric Reveal]
Blogger holds the shirt up against the window light, letting viewers see the fabric weave and texture. Expression is exaggerated excitement.
SFX: Natural fabric rustling.

(4-8s) [TEXTURE PROOF]
Roll up the sleeve, rub face against the shoulder fabric (proving it's not scratchy). Then flick the fabric with a finger to show the drape.
SFX: Fabric touch sounds add authenticity.

(8-11s) [FULL LOOK]
Untuck the shirt hem. Do a walking-with-the-wind move (or pretend to be at the beach). Show the full outfit in motion.
SFX: Footsteps + fabric flowing.

(11-15s) [CTA — Smile Close]
Adjust the collar facing camera. Flash a huge smile.
SFX: Natural room tone.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: Pure excitement energy — "Oh my gosh" opener grabs attention through enthusiasm
- Wow Moment: Rubbing face on fabric (tactile proof that linen isn't scratchy)
- Sound Strategy: Minimal BGM. Human voice dominates. Fabric sounds = authenticity
- Key Takeaway: Tone must be exaggerated — lots of vocal inflection, like showing off a haul find. Big movements: tug the shirt, spin, lean into camera, step back. The bigger the gestures, the more engaging the video

## Compression Socks 3-Pack · Product Review Flow · Urgency Hook

**Product**: Compression Socks 3-Pack | 15-20 mmHg pressure | Thick material
**Routine**: Hands-on review with stretch test — proving quality through physical demo
**BGM**: None or very subtle. Voice-forward
**Character**: American female, 20-30s, sitting on bed. Casual, like unboxing a just-delivered package
**Setting**: Bedroom, natural daylight. Sitting on bed — feels like sorting through a fresh delivery

```
Voiceover: "Stop! Don't buy the wrong compression socks. You have to get these specifically. Look at the difference. The material on these is actually thick and the stretch is insane. They give you that perfect fifteen to twenty millimeter pressure. No swelling, no sliding down. If you stand all day or travel, this is the three-pack you need. It's linked right here."

(0-3s) [HOOK — Urgency Stop]
Sitting on bed or couch, clutching the big bundle of black compression socks. Serious/urgent expression, waving hand at camera.
SFX: Natural room tone.

(3-7s) [STRETCH TEST — Physical Proof]
Pull out one sock. Stretch it hard in both directions with both hands, then snap-release — it instantly bounces back to shape.
SFX: Elastic stretch sound + snap-back.

(7-11s) [ON-BODY DEMO]
Quick cut to wearing them. Pat the calf muscle. Pull the top band to show it doesn't dig into skin.
SFX: Patting sound on fabric.

(11-15s) [CTA — Bundle Close]
Grab the whole bundle again. Lean into camera.
SFX: Natural tone.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: "Stop! Don't buy the wrong..." creates urgency and positions this as THE answer
- Wow Moment: Violent stretch test — compression socks all look the same, so the stretch is the differentiator
- Sound Strategy: Voice-only, no music. Feels like a friend urgently telling you before you make a mistake
- Key Takeaway: Hold a big handful of socks — the "bulk" visual communicates value. Stretch test is critical for compression products since they're hard to differentiate visually. Bedroom setting + natural light = authentic "just got my delivery" energy
