## Resolve Ultra Pet Spray · Before/After Flow · Pain Point Hook

**Product**: Resolve Ultra Pet Stain Spray | Lifts stains, destroys odors, prevents re-marking
**Routine**: Before/after cleaning demo — satisfying stain removal
**BGM**: None. Amplify spray "hiss" and wipe "whoosh" sounds
**Character**: POV hands only
**Setting**: Bright, high-key lighting on carpet

```
Voiceover: "Stop scrubbing pet messes like this. Just spray Resolve Ultra Pet. It lifts stains and destroys odors instantly. Stops them from marking the same spot. Carpet saved. Get yours now."

(0-3s) [HOOK — Failed Cleaning]
POV angle. A deep dark stain on carpet. Hand frantically scrubs with paper towel — the stain smears and spreads worse.
SFX: Rough, effortful scrubbing (sandpaper-like friction).

(3-6s) [PRODUCT ENTRY]
Red Resolve bottle enters frame. Press the spray trigger — aim directly at the stain. Close-up: white foam instantly covers and wraps around the stain.
SFX: Loud, crisp spray hiss (Psshh — Psshh).

(6-10s) [SATISFYING WIPE — Wow Moment]
Take a clean white towel. Slowly wipe through the center of the foam. Everywhere the towel passes, the carpet is instantly restored to white. All the stain transfers onto the towel.
SFX: Smooth wipe whoosh + sparkle chime (Ding!).

(10-13s) [PET PROOF]
A dog (or cat) walks to the freshly cleaned spot, sniffs once, then walks away uninterested — implies zero residual smell.
SFX: Paw steps + sniffing sound.

(13-15s) [BEFORE/AFTER]
Split screen: left half shows dirty carpet, right half shows pristine carpet. Product placed in the center. Frame freezes.
SFX: Rhythmic closing beat.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: Show the WRONG way first (scrubbing makes it worse) — viewer immediately relates
- Wow Moment: The single white-towel wipe revealing clean carpet underneath
- Sound Strategy: Zero BGM. Amplified real sounds: scrubbing friction → spray hiss → satisfying wipe. The audio journey mirrors frustration → solution → relief
- Key Takeaway: Pet walking away from the cleaned spot is subtle but powerful proof of odor elimination. The white towel is essential — it visually captures the transferred dirt

## TERRO Liquid Ant Bait · Tactical/Military Aesthetic · Pain Point Hook

**Product**: TERRO Liquid Ant Bait | 12-pack | Attracts then kills entire colony including queen
**Routine**: Problem → tactical solution — presented like deploying a weapon
**BGM**: Industrial rhythm, tactical low bass. Zero music for first 4 seconds
**Setting**: Kitchen counter, ant trail visible

```
Voiceover: "Stop spraying. You're fighting a losing battle. Use this instead. It acts like a magnet. They swarm the liquid bait instantly. They don't die here. They carry the poison back to the queen. Colony destroyed overnight. Twelve packs for total defense. Get it now."

(0-4s) [HOOK — Failed Method vs Weapon]
Quick flash: hand frantically spraying aerosol everywhere (red X overlay). Then a hand slaps the TERRO bait station onto the counter. Close-up: fingers snap off the bait station's activation tab.
SFX: Spray hiss → heavy slap on counter → crisp plastic snap (Snap!).

(4-8s) [VISUAL IMPACT — Ant Swarm]
Time-lapse: bait station placed on ant trail. A few scattered ants quickly become a dense swarm crowding around the liquid bait.
SFX: Subtle liquid flow + clock tick-tick (time acceleration).

(8-11s) [CORE LOGIC — Colony Kill]
Ants marching away from the bait in a line (carrying poison home). Optional text overlay or animation: ants delivering poison to the queen. Text: "KILLS THE QUEEN."
SFX: Tactical bass undertone.

(11-15s) [RESULT + CTA]
Next day: completely clean counter — zero ants. Quick toss of the 12-pack box into a cabinet corner. Point to link.
SFX: Sparkle chime (Ding!) + box shaking rattle.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: "Stop spraying" immediately calls out what viewers are doing wrong. The snap-off activation tab provides a tactile ASMR moment
- Wow Moment: Time-lapse ant swarm — disgusting but mesmerizing (high completion rate)
- Sound Strategy: Industrial/tactical music reinforces the "weapon deployment" narrative. First 4 seconds are silent for maximum impact
- Key Takeaway: The "kills the queen" narrative elevates this from a simple product to a strategic solution. The time-lapse is essential — it proves the product works in a visually undeniable way

## Nitrile Gloves · Multi-Scene Quick-Cut Flow · Humor Hook

**Product**: Nitrile Gloves | Medical-grade, powder-free, textured fingertips | 100/box
**Routine**: Multi-scenario showcase — same product, different use cases
**BGM**: Light country music or upbeat background
**Character**: American male, 30-40s, handy/DIY type. Confident with a touch of humor
**Setting**: Multiple quick-cut locations: garage, kitchen, bathroom

```
Voiceover: "Whoop whoop! Just kidding. But seriously, these gloves are legit. Medical grade, powder-free. I use 'em for fixing my truck, prepping BBQ, and scrubbing the nasty stuff. Look at that stretch! They don't rip, and the grip is insane. Hundred per box, crazy cheap right now. Link is right here, go get some."

(0-3s) [HOOK — Snap & Humor]
Pull up a glove on one hand, stretch the cuff, snap-release with a loud pop. Do a mock-serious doctor gesture (two fingers together). Then break into a grin.
SFX: Loud elastic snap (SNAP!).

(3-7s) [MULTI-SCENE QUICK CUTS]
Rapid three-shot sequence: 1. Hands gripping an oil-covered wrench (garage/mechanic). 2. Handling raw meat / seasoning BBQ (kitchen). 3. Holding a toilet brush / cleaning spray (bathroom).
SFX: Quick transition whooshes between scenes.

(7-11s) [STRETCH TEST]
Pull hard on the glove fingers — stretch them extremely long, then release. Gloves snap back intact. Close-up: textured fingertip surface detail.
SFX: Rubber tension stretch sound.

(11-15s) [CTA — Bulk Value]
Slap a stack of multiple glove boxes. Point to the link below.
SFX: Box slap sound.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: The glove snap is universally recognizable + funny fake-doctor bit breaks the ice
- Wow Moment: Extreme stretch without tearing — visually proves durability
- Sound Strategy: Light background music + natural sounds. The snap is the audio hook
- Key Takeaway: Multi-scenario quick cuts communicate versatility without needing to explain each use case. "Hundred per box" hits value. The humor makes an otherwise boring product memorable
