## Kaytee Ultimate No-Mess Bird Food · Problem/Solution Flow · Education Hook

**Product**: Kaytee Ultimate No Mess Bird Food | No hulls, no shells | Nuts & fruit blend
**Routine**: Problem education → product swap → visual result
**BGM**: None. Natural bird sounds as ambient soundtrack
**Setting**: Backyard, bird feeder area, natural daylight

```
Voiceover: "Birds throwing seeds everywhere? That's because they hate the cheap red fillers. Switch to Kaytee Ultimate. No hulls, no shells, just nuts and fruit. It attracts the colorful birds you actually want to see. Zero waste means zero rodents. Stop paying for dirt. Link here."

(0-5s) [HOOK — The Mess Problem]
Camera on the ground below a bird feeder — pile of discarded red seeds scattered everywhere. Big red X overlay.
SFX: Buzzer / error sound.

(5-9s) [PRODUCT SWAP]
Slam the Kaytee Ultimate bag onto the frame. Quick zoom into the "NO MESS" and "Nut & Fruit" text on packaging. Grab a handful — show: all sunflower seed kernels, peanuts, and dried fruit pieces.
SFX: Bag thud + seeds pouring (satisfying ASMR).

(9-12s) [VISUAL PAYOFF — Birds Feeding]
A cardinal or blue jay eating enthusiastically from the feeder — eating everything clean, no waste dropping.
SFX: Clear, cheerful birdsong.

(12-15s) [RESULT + CTA]
Clean ground below feeder — no mess, no rodent bait. Point to purchase link.
SFX: Cash register ding.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: "They hate the cheap red fillers" — educates viewers on WHY the mess happens, not just that it does
- Wow Moment: Colorful birds (cardinal, blue jay) feeding — bird lovers can't scroll past this
- Sound Strategy: Natural bird sounds throughout. No music needed — birdsong IS the soundtrack
- Key Takeaway: "Zero waste = zero rodents" connects two problems with one solution. The education angle ("they hate cheap fillers") positions the viewer as newly informed, making them more likely to buy

## Benebone Wishbone Dog Chew · Pet Destruction Flow · Humor Hook

**Product**: Benebone Wishbone | Real bacon infused | Curved grip design | Ultra-durable
**Routine**: Problem (destroyed stuff) → solution → happy pet + happy owner
**BGM**: Funk/upbeat drums. Intersperse with dog chewing ASMR. Light and fun ending
**Character**: Dog owner, slightly exasperated humor ("finally found a solution")
**Setting**: Living room / home with dog

```
Voiceover: "Stop letting your dog eat your shoes. They're just bored. Give them a Benebone. Made with REAL bacon and tougher than rocks. Curved shape so they can actually hold it. Look at that grip. Hours of silence. Your furniture will thank you. Get it below."

(0-4s) [HOOK — Destruction Scene]
Show a chewed-up slipper or pile of destroyed plush toy remnants. Red X overlay. Cut to the dog's innocent, bored expression.
SFX: Alert buzzer / scratch sound.

(4-9s) [PRODUCT INTRO — Hardness Demo]
Slam the Benebone hard on the table/floor twice to demonstrate hardness. Close-up: "REAL BACON" text on packaging. Person holds it near their nose and makes an "mmm, smells good" expression.
SFX: Hard impact sounds (BANG! BANG!) + sniffing inhale.

(9-12s) [DOG USING IT — Design Showcase]
Toss the bone to the dog. Key shot: dog presses one end with a paw while the other end lifts up for easy chewing. Show the dog intensely focused on gnawing.
SFX: ASMR chewing sounds (crunch-crunch).

(12-15s) [HAPPY ENDING]
Dog peacefully chewing in the corner. Camera pans across intact furniture and shoes. Point to link.
SFX: Angelic choir / sparkle chime.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: Destroyed shoes/toys — every dog owner relates instantly. The bored dog face adds humor
- Wow Moment: Dog gripping the curved bone with its paw — the design feature demonstrated naturally
- Sound Strategy: Funk beat creates playful energy. Dog ASMR chewing is surprisingly satisfying. Angelic choir at the peaceful ending = comedic payoff
- Key Takeaway: "REAL bacon" is the trust signal — dogs actually want this. The before/after (destruction vs peace) sells the outcome, not the product. "Hours of silence" is the real value proposition for owners

## Building Blocks 80-Piece Set · Parent Lifestyle Flow · Relatable Hook

**Product**: Building Blocks 80-Piece Set | Large pieces for small hands | Comes in zippered storage bag
**Routine**: Parent hack — "buy yourself 30 minutes of peace"
**BGM**: Light, cheerful ukulele or acoustic guitar
**Character**: Parent (voice), toddler (on camera). Warm, loving tone — chatting with friends
**Setting**: Living room floor, natural daylight. Low camera angle at child's eye level

```
Voiceover: "If you need thirty minutes of peace, this bag is your best friend. Eighty pieces, perfect size for little hands. Super easy to stack and pull apart. It helps with motor skills and keeps them busy away from screens. And the best part? Cleanup takes two seconds. Linked it for you guys!"

(0-4s) [HOOK — The Pour]
Dump the entire bag of blocks onto the carpet — blocks cascade out in a colorful avalanche. Child excitedly rushes over.
SFX: Blocks cascading out (satisfying clatter — this is the KEY ASMR moment).

(4-8s) [SMALL HANDS CLOSE-UP]
Close-up of toddler's chubby fingers grabbing a block and pressing it onto another. Show the bright colors and easy snap-fit.
SFX: Blocks clicking together.

(8-11s) [CREATIVITY & FOCUS]
Child builds a wobbly tall tower, then gleefully pushes it over (or show their focused, concentrating expression).
SFX: Tower tumble + child giggling.

(11-15s) [CLEANUP MAGIC]
Sweep all blocks back into the blue zippered bag. Pull the zipper shut. Lift and shake the bag — done.
SFX: Zipper pull + bag shake rattle.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: "30 minutes of peace" — speaks directly to the parent's real desire, not the child's
- Wow Moment: The big pour — blocks cascading onto carpet is visually and audibly satisfying
- Sound Strategy: Block clatter ASMR is the hero sound. Light acoustic BGM stays under the natural sounds. Child laughter is authentic gold
- Key Takeaway: Film at LOW ANGLE (child's eye level) for intimate perspective. The zippered bag cleanup is the "parent hack" — it sells convenience. "Away from screens" hits the modern parenting guilt point without being preachy
