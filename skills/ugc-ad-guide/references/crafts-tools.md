## ARTISTRO Acrylic Markers · Multi-Surface Demo Flow · Comparison Hook

**Product**: ARTISTRO Dual-Tip Acrylic Markers | Fine tip + dot tip | Works on rock, wood, glass, canvas
**Routine**: Old mess vs new tool → multi-surface rapid demo → finished results
**BGM**: Beat-synced music for the multi-surface montage. Quiet during close-ups
**Character**: POV hands only (first-person overhead angle)
**Setting**: Craft table, bright natural side-light or high-CRI desk lamp

```
Voiceover: "Stop struggling with messy paint brushes and leaky tubes! These dual-tip acrylic markers are a total game changer for any DIY project. They work on almost any surface — like rocks, wood, and even glass — with zero bleed and super vibrant colors. They're quick-drying and so convenient. Grab your set at the link in my bio!"

(0-3s) [HOOK — Mess Comparison]
Overhead POV: messy paint palette, dirty brush water, and squeezed-out paint tubes scattered on desk. A hand shoves all the mess off-screen. A neatly packaged box of colorful dual-tip markers slides into frame center.
SFX: Clutter pushing sound.

(3-7s) [DUAL-TIP DEMO — Ink Quality]
Extreme close-up: uncap the pen. Fine tip draws a bold, opaque line on a dark black stone — zero transparency, full coverage. Quickly flip the pen. Dot tip stamps three perfect circles next to it: tap-tap-tap.
SFX: Marker on stone (satisfying scratch) + rhythmic tapping.

(7-11s) [MULTI-SURFACE SPEED MONTAGE]
Beat-synced rapid cuts: 1. Bold color stroke on a rough wood slice. 2. Delicate flower drawn on a clear glass mug. 3. Graffiti-style doodle on canvas sneakers. Zero bleeding, vivid pigment on every surface.
SFX: Music-synced hits matching each cut.

(11-15s) [FINISHED RESULTS + CTA]
Camera pulls back. Beautifully painted stone, wood slice, and glass mug arranged together. Hand picks up the marker box, taps it with satisfaction, then points to the link below.
SFX: Satisfying tap + closing chime.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: Messy traditional art supplies shoved away — instantly relatable for crafters
- Wow Moment: The opaque line on black stone — proving coverage quality in one stroke
- Sound Strategy: Marker-on-surface ASMR for close-ups, beat-synced music for the montage. The rhythmic tapping is uniquely satisfying
- Key Takeaway: Multi-surface montage communicates versatility better than words. Overhead POV is the standard craft TikTok angle. "Zero bleed" must be SHOWN, not just said
