## Description:

Assists with outlines, drafting, revision, review, evidence checks, and citation checks for Chinese undergraduate papers, master's theses, course papers, proposals, and literature reviews using user-provided or explicitly authorized sources.

This skill is ready for commercial/non-commercial use.

## Publisher:

## Use Case:

Students, researchers, and academic writers use this skill to develop and review Chinese academic manuscripts while preserving source boundaries, citation traceability, and institutional academic-integrity requirements.

### Deployment Geography for Use:

Global

## Known Risks and Mitigations:

Risk: Academic work could be misused to obscure authorship or create unsupported core contributions.

Mitigation: Use the skill with author-provided or explicitly authorized materials, preserve source boundaries, and keep the author responsible for institutional academic-integrity requirements.

Risk: Drafts or reviews could include unsupported claims, fabricated evidence, or citation mismatches.

Mitigation: Require evidence checks against available source text, metadata, and citation support; keep unverified sources out of final prose.

Risk: Optional research, state files, or local audits could exceed the user's intended scope.

Mitigation: Authorize web research and .academic-writing state files only when the scope is acceptable, and run read-only audits only on files the user names.
