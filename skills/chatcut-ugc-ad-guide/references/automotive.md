## Microfiber Leather Steering Wheel Cover · POV Quality ASMR Flow · Pain Point Hook

**Product**: Microfiber Leather Steering Wheel Cover | Carbon fiber texture | Anti-slip, sweat-absorbing
**Routine**: POV first-person — tactile pain point → install → driving payoff
**BGM**: None for first 8s (pure ASMR). Engine + coin sound at CTA

```
Voiceover: "Stop driving with a slippery, boring steering wheel! Check this out. This isn't just a cover. It's Microfiber Leather with anti-slip texture. It takes ten seconds to install, and look at that fit. It absorbs sweat and stays locked in place. Instant grip upgrade. It makes your car feel brand new. Link is right there."

(0-3s) [HOOK — Slippery Pain Point]
POV from driver seat. Hands slide on a worn, shiny steering wheel — fingers slip off.
SFX: Slippery squeak sound (Squeak).

(3-8s) [PRODUCT REVEAL — Texture ASMR]
Unbox the cover. Close-up: carbon fiber texture and breathable holes. Fingers scratch the surface hard — satisfying grip sound.
SFX: Fingernail scratch on textured surface (Scratch-Scratch). Emphasize material quality through sound.

(8-13s) [INSTALL — Speed Ramp]
Speed ramp: snap it on in one swift motion. Close-up edge — zero gaps. Twist hard with both hands — doesn't budge at all.
SFX: Install snap (Snap!) — crisp and satisfying.

(13-18s) [RESULT — Driving Shot]
Driving shot: confident one-hand turn on a curve. Pull back to full steering wheel gleaming.
SFX: Engine roar (Vroom) + coin sound (Cha-ching).
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: Tactile pain point (hand slipping) is more visceral than visual — viewers feel it
- Wow Moment: Hard twist after install that doesn't budge — proves the grip claim
- Sound Strategy: Pure ASMR for first 8 seconds (scratch, snap). Engine sounds only at the driving payoff
- Key Takeaway: Full POV creates immersive experience. "It makes your car feel brand new" is a powerful emotional close. The scratch ASMR on carbon fiber texture is the audio hook

## Mobil 1 Extended Performance Synthetic Oil · DIY Tutorial Flow · Price Shock Hook

**Product**: Mobil 1 Extended Performance | Synthetic motor oil | 20,000-mile protection
**Routine**: DIY money-saving tutorial — mechanical process showcase
**BGM**: None. Pure mechanical ASMR throughout
**Character**: American male, car enthusiast / DIY mechanic type. High energy, confident
**Setting**: Garage or driveway, car on jack stands

```
Voiceover: "Stop paying a hundred dollars for an oil change! Do it yourself in fifteen minutes. Drain the old oil, swap the filter, plug it back up. It's easier than making breakfast. Here's the secret sauce: Mobil 1 Extended Performance. It protects for twenty thousand miles! That's a whole year of driving. Check the dipstick. Perfect gold color. Your engine stays clean, and your wallet stays full. Get the best oil right here. Your car will thank you."

(0-3s) [HOOK — Price Shock]
Show a repair shop bill ($80+). Crumple it up and throw it away dramatically.
SFX: Paper crumple + throw sound.

(3-8s) [PROCESS — Quick DIY Montage]
Fast cuts of three actions: 1. Unscrew the drain plug — black used oil flows out. 2. Swap in a new oil filter. 3. Tighten the drain plug back.
SFX: Ratchet wrench sounds + liquid glugging.

(8-10s) [PRODUCT HERO — The Pour]
Hold the Mobil 1 jug. Pour into the engine fill port. Close-up: crystal-clear golden oil flowing in.
Camera: Must capture the clean golden transparency of fresh synthetic oil.
SFX: Smooth liquid pour.

(10-13s) [RESULT CHECK]
Pull out the dipstick — oil level perfect, beautiful gold color. Close the hood.
SFX: Dipstick slide (Schwing) + hood slam (Thud).

(13-15s) [CTA]
Pat the oil jug. Place it on the car roof or toolbox. Arrow points to purchase link.
SFX: Pat sound + subtle chime.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: "$100 oil change" price shock — every car owner has felt this pain
- Wow Moment: The golden oil pour — clean, translucent, premium-looking liquid is visually satisfying
- Sound Strategy: Full mechanical ASMR — wrench clicks, oil glugging, dipstick slide. These are sounds car enthusiasts love
- Key Takeaway: "20,000 miles = a whole year" translates a spec into real-life terms. DIY tutorials build trust because viewers learn something valuable. "Easier than making breakfast" lowers the intimidation barrier
