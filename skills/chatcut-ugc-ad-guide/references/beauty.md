## Hand Warmers · Seasonal/Lifestyle Flow · Pain Point Hook

**Product**: Hand Warmers | 10-pair value pack | $7 | 10-hour heat, TSA approved
**Routine**: Seasonal lifestyle — cold weather pain point → warm cozy solution
**BGM**: None or ambient winter sounds. ASMR-forward
**Setting**: Cold winter outdoor, frosty breath visible, natural daylight

```
Voiceover: "Stop freezing your hands off every winter. Rip one open, shake it, and ten hours of heat right in your pocket. Ten pairs for seven bucks. TSA approved, fits anywhere. I shove these in my jacket, my gloves, even my boots. Total lifesaver. Grab yours before winter's over."

(0-3s) [HOOK — Cold Hands]
Close-up: bare red hands rubbing together in cold air. Visible frosty breath. Fingers stiff and shaking.
SFX: Wind chill ambient sound.

(3-5s) [PRODUCT ACTIVATION]
Rip open a small white packet with a satisfying tear. Shake it twice.
SFX: Packet tear (Riiip) + shaking rattle.

(5-11s) [DEMO — Multi-Use]
Tuck the warmer into a glove, slide hand in. Close-up: hand relaxes with warmth. Pull out more packets from the big orange value pack — fan them out like playing cards. Stuff one in jacket pocket, one in boot.
SFX: Fabric rustling + packet crinkling.

(11-13s) [PROOF — Warm & Cozy]
Hands in gloves gripping a hot coffee cup outdoors, relaxed and warm. Steam rising.
SFX: Gentle outdoor ambient.

(13-15s) [CTA — Pack Shot]
Pull back: the orange value pack sitting on a snowy surface, frost around it.
SFX: Soft closing tone.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: Physical discomfort (red shaking hands) is instantly relatable in winter
- Wow Moment: Fanning out the packets like playing cards — communicates abundance and value
- Sound Strategy: Zero BGM. Pure winter ambient + ASMR packet sounds. The silence makes the cold feel real
- Key Takeaway: Seasonal products need urgency ("before winter's over"). "TSA approved" is a hidden killer feature — travelers immediately perk up. $7 for 10 pairs anchors extreme value

## Essence Mascara · Price Comparison Flow · Value Hook

**Product**: Essence Lash Princess Mascara | $4.99 | Conic fiber brush, cruelty-free
**Routine**: Drugstore gem vs luxury — price shock comparison
**BGM**: Upbeat Pop with strong rhythm
**Character**: Female, 20-30s. "Hidden treasure" excitement energy. Fast-paced, energetic delivery
**Setting**: Vanity/bathroom mirror, bright lighting for eye close-ups

```
Voiceover: "Stop wasting money! This five-dollar drugstore gem beats high-end brands. Look at this volume! No falsies, just the conic fiber brush doing its magic. It lengthens instantly, never clumps, AND it's one hundred percent cruelty-free. Cheaper than your morning latte. Run, don't walk! Link below."

(0-3s) [HOOK — Price Shock]
Split-screen effect: left side "$30," right side "$4.99." Hand dramatically tosses a blurred luxury mascara into the trash, then slams the green Essence tube onto the table.
SFX: Trash can clunk + heavy slam (Boom).

(3-7s) [PROOF — Half-Face Demo]
Extreme macro shot: face still, left eye bare (short lashes), right eye with dramatic volume (spider-leg density). Finger points to the done eye. Quick flash of the brush head close-up.
SFX: Magic sparkle + gasp sound.

(7-11s) [PROCESS]
Fast cuts: 1. Side-angle Z-stroke brush application (smooth, no clumping). 2. Close-up of the cruelty-free bunny logo on the tube.
SFX: Brush swish + ding notification.

(11-15s) [CTA — Stock Up]
Hand clutching three mascara tubes, shaking them at camera. Finger frantically tapping the shopping cart area at bottom of screen.
SFX: Cash register (Cha-ching) + fast drumbeat.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: Price split-screen is TikTok's most effective beauty format — "$30 vs $5" stops the scroll
- Wow Moment: Half-face comparison — one bare eye vs one dramatic eye is undeniable proof
- Sound Strategy: Upbeat pop rhythm matches the energetic "I found a treasure" vibe. SFX punctuate key moments
- Key Takeaway: Drugstore beauty needs "treasure hunter" energy — the excitement of finding something amazing for cheap. Holding 3 tubes = stocking up = social proof ("it's so good I bought multiples")

## Hydrogel Mask · Transformation Flow · Visual Curiosity Hook

**Product**: Hydrogel Sleeping Mask | Solid gel, turns transparent overnight, dissolves into body serum
**Routine**: Before/after transformation — visual magic of transparency change
**BGM**: Calming/spa background, building to reveal
**Character**: American female, 20-30s, skincare enthusiast
**Setting**: Bedroom/bathroom, soft warm lighting

```
Voiceover: "Watch this mask turn transparent as it absorbs overnight! It's solid hydrogel, not paper. It pushes collagen DEEP into your pores while you sleep. The peel-off is so satisfying... Look at that GLOW! Literally glass skin. Plus, dissolve it for body serum! Zero waste. Grab yours at the link!"

(0-4s) [HOOK — Color Change Reveal]
Split screen: Left — freshly applied pure white, thick mask. Right — morning after, completely transparent thin film. Large text overlay: "WHITE → CLEAR?"
SFX: Rewind sound + ding.

(4-8s) [MECHANISM — How It Works]
Fast cuts: 1. Finger presses the transparent film on face (showing jelly-like texture). 2. Side-lying sleeping shot (mask stays on, doesn't fall off).
SFX: Water ripple sound + subtle snore.

(8-12s) [REVEAL — Peel-Off Moment]
Extreme close-up: slowly peel off the transparent mask. Underneath: glowing, egg-shell smooth skin. Finger pokes cheek — bouncy and plump.
SFX: ASMR peel sound + angelic choir (Hallelujah).

(12-15s) [BONUS + CTA]
Lightning-fast: 1. Drop the used mask into warm water cup (0.5s). 2. Hold product, point to link.
SFX: Splash + cash register (Cha-ching).
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: "White → Clear?" visual mystery — viewers NEED to know how this works
- Wow Moment: The peel-off reveal of glowing skin underneath
- Sound Strategy: Calm/spa tone builds to the angelic choir at the reveal — emotional arc through audio
- Key Takeaway: Skincare products that visually transform are TikTok gold. The dissolve-into-serum bonus adds "zero waste" appeal. Glass skin terminology resonates with the K-beauty audience

## Kojic Acid + Turmeric Soap · Ingredient Focus Flow · Pain Point Hook

**Product**: Kojic Acid + Turmeric Soap | Fades dark spots, evens skin tone | Comes with free African bathing net
**Routine**: Problem → lather ASMR → result
**BGM**: Fresh, light ASMR intro with bubble sounds, then transition to light R&B
**Character**: Female model, clear skin. "Glow-up" energy
**Setting**: Bathroom, bright even lighting for skin visibility

```
Voiceover: "Dark spots? Meet the Glow Bomb! Kojic Acid plus Turmeric. Fades dark spots and evens skin tone. Safe for face and body. Plus, a FREE African Net! Get your glow now! Link below!"

(0-3s) [HOOK — Pain Point Close-Up]
Camera close on skin (knee, elbow, or underarm area), finger pointing at dark patches. Quick cut to hand holding the orange soap bar.
SFX: Rewind/question sound effect.

(3-8s) [LATHER ASMR — Satisfying Demo]
Rub the soap on the included red African bathing net. Instantly generate extremely rich, dense white foam. Close-up of the creamy foam texture.
SFX: Scrubbing sound + satisfying foam bubbles.

(8-12s) [APPLICATION — Multi-Area]
Quick flashes of foam applied to arms, inner thighs, and bikini area (suggestive angles, tasteful). Show rinsing — skin looks dewy and glowing.
SFX: Water rinse + skin gleaming.

(12-15s) [CTA — Glow Result]
Model holds the soap box near face, smiling. Skin catching the light with visible glow. Finger points to shopping cart below.
SFX: Cash register (Cha-ching!).
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: "Dark spots?" is a two-word pain point that immediately filters the right audience
- Wow Moment: The dense foam explosion from the African net — extremely satisfying lather ASMR
- Sound Strategy: ASMR foam sounds dominate the first half, then light R&B carries the result reveal
- Key Takeaway: The free African net is both a bonus and a content device — it creates the foam ASMR moment. "Safe for face & body" broadens the use case without extra demo time
