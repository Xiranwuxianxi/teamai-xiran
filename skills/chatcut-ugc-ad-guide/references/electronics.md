## JBL Charge 6 · ASMR Product Close-Up Flow · No-Voiceover Showcase

**Product**: JBL Charge 6 | Waterproof speaker | Passive radiators | Braided mesh | Strap loop
**Routine**: Pure ASMR product showcase — texture, water test, bass demo, zero voiceover
**BGM**: Strong-rhythm Funk/Pop or beat-synced drums. Music IS the content

```
(No voiceover — music + sound effects only. Text overlays: "Charge 6 / Link in Bio" at end.)

(0-2s) [LOGO REVEAL]
Extreme close-up of the orange/red JBL logo on the speaker face. Camera rapidly zooms out to reveal the full speaker.
SFX: Whoosh (zoom-out) + BGM heavy downbeat.

(2-4s) [TEXTURE ASMR]
Close-up of the braided mesh grille. Fingernail quickly scrapes across the weave — rough, tactile texture.
SFX: Scraping sound + BGM rhythm.

(4-6s) [BASS WATER TEST]
Show the side passive radiators (the vibrating discs). Pour a little water on the radiator. Play heavy bass — water droplets explode and dance off the surface.
SFX: Deep bass boom + water splash.

(6-8s) [WATERPROOF TEST]
Dump an entire cup of water directly onto the speaker. Or submerge it in a full sink. Slow-motion for the splash moment. Speaker keeps playing.
SFX: Plunge / splash sound.

(8-10s) [STRAP SPIN]
Hook a finger through the strap loop. Spin the speaker in the air — one full rotation showing the strap's strength.
SFX: Wind swish.

(10-12s) [PLAY BUTTON]
Close-up of the raised rubber buttons on top. Thumb presses the play button firmly.
SFX: Tactile click + music volume jumps louder.

(12-15s) [HERO SHOT]
Speaker sitting on outdoor grass or rocks. Golden sunlight. Text: "Charge 6 / Link in Bio."
SFX: BGM closes out.
```

**Breakdown**:

- Hook: Extreme close-up → zoom-out reveal — the visual surprise of scale
- Wow Moment: Water droplets dancing off the passive radiator during bass — mesmerizing physics
- Sound Strategy: No voiceover. BGM IS the product demo (it's a speaker). ASMR textures between beats
- Key Takeaway: Speakers should be demonstrated through their sound. The water test serves double duty: proves waterproofing AND creates a viral visual moment
