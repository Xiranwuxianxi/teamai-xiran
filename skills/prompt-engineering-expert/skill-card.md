## Known Risks and Mitigations: <br>
Risk: A refusal-troubleshooting example may encourage reframing requests in ways that bypass legitimate model safety refusals. <br>
Mitigation: Before use in safety-sensitive settings, revise or ignore that example and keep prompt guidance aligned with legitimate, policy-compliant use cases. <br>
Risk: Prompt recommendations may be incomplete or unsuitable for critical applications if used without testing. <br>
Mitigation: Validate recommendations against real use cases, review outputs before deployment, and keep human judgment in the approval path. <br>
